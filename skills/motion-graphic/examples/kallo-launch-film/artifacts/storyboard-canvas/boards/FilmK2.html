How the product enters

A match cut on a shape: the ring made of food and the ring in the app are the same size in the same place, so the food literally turns into the product.

Decisions for you

The figure
PickThe ring: ownable, and it needs no numbers for each kitchen
OrEach meal’s own kcal number: needs a real capture per meal
Material
PickTop-down cut-outs laid on paper, stop-motion on twos
OrMacro food surfaces whose edges trace the arc: the most premium look, the hardest to generate
Grounds
PickOne step-blob colour per kitchen: terracotta, sage, violet, sky; the last ring on the brand sweep
OrOne cream paper throughout

What we make

Codex: ~40 top-down ingredient cut-outs in one lighting set (the pipeline already made 20). Remotion packs them along the arc by code, so every ring is dense and gap-free. Data: numbers only on the final meal (548 kcal, real).
