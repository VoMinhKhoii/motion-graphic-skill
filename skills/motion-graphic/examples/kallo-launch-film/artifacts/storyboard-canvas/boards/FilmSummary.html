Can Codex make the imagery?

Yes, for everything that is a picture: top-down food cut-outs with real transparency (20 made so far), plated dishes, macro food surfaces, paper and fabric grounds.

Every brief says no people, no hands, no text, and states the app’s palette; each image is checked before it is used, and the grade is finished in the edit.

Not the UI: the iPhone and web screens are rebuilt in code from the real app, with real data, so they stay sharp and true.

Not the type or the numbers: the type is set in Be Vietnam Pro in the edit, and every number comes from a real pipeline run.

Transitions are code, built from the cards (the dot iris, the shelf, the outline morph), on top of the gradients above.

Weakest spot: macro close-ups sharp enough for full-screen. The cut-out route (K1, K2, K5) is safe; macro needs a test first.

My pick

Launch film: K1. It is the product truth in 20 s, and both platforms land naturally. If you want more spectacle, its tiles can collapse into K2’s ring of food instead of a dot.

Launch week: K2 the day before, as a teaser with no UI; K1 on launch day; K5 as the follow-up post that asks for replies. K3 is the calmest and needs fresh captures; K4 needs measured timings.
