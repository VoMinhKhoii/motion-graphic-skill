One day, the way you ate it

One Thursday, told by the light. The film never cuts away from the product: the colour of the world is the time of day, a clock in the corner rolls from 8:12 AM to 10:40 PM, and every hour is one thing Kallo does. Breakfast is typed the way it was eaten and parses on screen; the same breakfast typed without the details comes out 169 kcal lighter. A granola bar is scanned from its label. Lunch is typed on the web, the same day in the same account. Friends’ meals pass by in Circle. A large pizza is logged whole, the day runs over, and splitting it with two friends gives most of it back: your 5 of 20 parts, 614 kcal. At night the day lands at 1,865 of a 1,932 kcal target, with the vitamins behind it. Friday morning, one “/” logs yesterday’s breakfast again, and the day’s ring closes behind the name.
