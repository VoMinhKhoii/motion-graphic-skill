Found along the way, for you

Bug · label OCR drops US calories: a "Calories 180" row has no unit, and normalizeAmount (lib/ai/pipeline/estimator/label-ocr/units.ts:116) only accepts kcal/cal/kJ, so it returns null. One line: treat an empty unit on calories as kcal.

Gap · salt is not converted to sodium: the UK/EU label’s "Salt 0.24 g" came back as sodium null (sodium mg = salt g × 400).

Variance · the same breakfast sentence returned 436 and 506 kcal on two dev runs; the film uses 506.

Copy · "kcal left" means your daily budget on the ring and the part you keep on the share button, three seconds apart in this film. Something like "· 614 kcal for you" on the button would read cleaner.

Copy · the iPhone composer says "Add a meal…", the web composer "Describe your meal..."; the film shows both as shipped.
