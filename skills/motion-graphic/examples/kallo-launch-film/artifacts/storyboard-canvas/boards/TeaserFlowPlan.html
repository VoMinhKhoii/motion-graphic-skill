How it's built, beat by beat
Home
A screenshot of the real Home, taken just before the take (the recorder drops still screens).
Log tab
Tapped by the pointer. The page push is rebuilt from the real Home and Log frames, as the app animates it.
Typing
Real, a letter at a time, sped up about 14×. Vietnamese is typed through the Vietnamese Telex keyboard, so tones land as they're typed.
Send → rows
Two status words at most, then the rows land at real speed (Vietnamese at 3×: it has seven dishes).
Zoom out → Save
The camera pulls back to the whole phone, the pointer taps Save (Vietnamese scrolls to the total first).
Gauge
The camera moves to the day's gauge as calories left drop and protein, carbs and fat fill, with the app's "Meal saved" toast.
End
Fade to black. The Kallo wordmark and one line: "Coming soon" (English), "Sắp ra mắt" (Vietnamese). No date.
Sound
Keys, taps, the send, a pop per row, the save chime, a soft low hit on the wordmark. The launch film's music sits low under it all and fades with the picture.
To decide

The Vietnamese end line. It reads "Sắp ra mắt" (Vietnamese for coming soon) so the whole cut stays in one language. Say if you'd rather keep the English "Coming soon" on both.

The music. The launch film's track sits low under the teaser so the two sound like one campaign. It can go SFX-only for a rawer feel.
