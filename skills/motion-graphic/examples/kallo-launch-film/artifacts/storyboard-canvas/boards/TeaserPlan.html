How A ends · "hard cut" vs "hold"

"Hard cut" means the video doesn't linger on the result. The total settles on its final number, stays for 0.2 s (just long enough to catch), and the picture jumps straight to black with no fade or zoom. It feels like the recording was cut off, which is the leaked feel. "Hold" keeps the total on screen for 0.7 s so it's easy to read, then cuts. Both are on the A board.
