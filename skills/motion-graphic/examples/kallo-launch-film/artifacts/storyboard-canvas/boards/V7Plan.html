K3 v7 · storyboard

One day, v7: every frame

72 s, built long on purpose so we can trim. Every UI pixel is the real dev build (simulator and web recordings, plus screenshots for the Circle feed and the nutrient tiles). Frames below are rendered from the actual edit at 4:5.

Your ask
What v7 does
Tracking camera + visible clicks ("real-time cursor tracking", esp. the share)
A spring camera chases a touch pointer the way screen-recording tools follow a cursor. Every tap presses and ripples; the split handles are dragged on camera; the web gets the macOS arrow.
Component morphing, not only whole-device
Composer lifted out → careless card → detailed card → docks into the phone; faces → posts; nutrient tiles → wall → back into the phone; Home ring → logo. Plus the phone → browser container.
The texts
"Introducing Kallo" · "The text-first nutrition tracker, built for precision." · "A nutrition tracker that… pays attention to details." · "From barcode scans… to nutrition label reading." · "At Kallo, we believe every journey works out better when we all feel supported. Especially dieting." · "We make it easy for you to relog a meal." · "Shared a pizza? Log your slice." · "Not just calories and macros."
Careless vs detailed: just the meal cards, then the whole screen
Both cards are real pipeline runs on the dev build: 282 kcal vs 504 kcal (+222). Only the cards, at hero size, until the dock.
Circle: no reload, posts one by one, more people
Five friends with photos (Maya, Leo, Sofia, Noah, Chloe). The feed is rebuilt from the real feed's own pixels so each post drops in, no pull-to-refresh; the like is the real recording.
Micronutrients less boring
The tiles burst into a wall, bars draw, then fly home.
Text screens more eye-catching
The onboarding's own light (StartAurora sweep + StepBackdrop blobs, real colours), moving; a new layout per screen.
Less vacant rooms
All six rooms regenerated lived-in, same light and palette.
Whole phone hard to read
Demo scenes run zoomed (0.6–1.2× bezel scale) on the part that matters; the whole phone only for a beat.
Sound like the OneNotch intro
125 BPM electronic bed (Mixkit "Forbidden", drop on the wordmark) + licensed SFX on every event. See below.
