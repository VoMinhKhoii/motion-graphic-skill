# Assets

Kallo's own material only: its renders, storyboard frames of its own film, and backgrounds generated for the project. Nothing here comes from a reference film. Total size about 104 MB.

## films/

The delivered film, as a "light" copy (x264 CRF 22, `-tune grain`, AAC audio). The CRF 14 masters are not included, and neither are the 4:5 and 9:16 versions of the same film or the two 9:16 teasers (kept out to keep the skill small).

| File | Size | Format | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kallo_v9d_16x9_light.mp4` | 32 MB | 1920x1080, 60 fps, 83.1 s | The final launch film, for X and the web. |

The films contain music and sound effects licensed from Pixabay and Mixkit, mixed into the soundtrack. Those licences allow the sounds inside a video like this one, but not as separate files, so the audio is not provided on its own anywhere in this repo.

The demo account in the films is the owner's: the app's greeting shows its first name, and the Circle scene shows the owner's profile photo, which the owner asked to include.

## storyboard/

Frames rendered from the teaser's own edit for the sign-off storyboards, 540x960 JPEG. The first three are from the three teaser directions, built on stand-in footage before the owner chose a flow. The rest are from the storyboard of the owner's flow (teaser v2). That version was slower than the final cut, which was sped up to 12.4 s (English) and 10.6 s (Vietnamese); the English meal and its numbers are the same as in the final.

| File | What it shows |

## rooms/

The six room backgrounds of the "Places" look, one per time of day. Generated with an image model for this project (briefs: no people, no hands, no text, the app's warm palette, lived-in), 1536x1024 JPEG. In the film they sit behind the phone, scaled up slightly and blurred by 6 px.

| File | Time of day | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `dawn_kitchen.jpg` | Dawn | A kitchen counter at sunrise, avocado toast on a plate. |
| `morning_desk.jpg` | Morning | A light wooden desk with notebooks and a coffee cup. |
| `noon_desk.jpg` | Noon | A desk with a laptop and a packed lunch. Also the teaser's room. |
| `afternoon_pantry.jpg` | Afternoon | A table set for a small celebration, cake and cups. |
| `evening_table.jpg` | Evening | A dinner table with a pizza box and candles, for the shared pizza. |
| `night_room.jpg` | Night | A living room by lamplight, a city at night outside. |
