# The research, distilled

What the worked example learned from its reference films, kept here because none of it is about Kallo: it is how high-fidelity product launch films and pre-launch teasers are built, measured. Use it as a head start for Step 4 and 5 of `SKILL.md`, and as baselines to compare your own film against. The per-film links and numbers are one level up (`../launch-films.csv`, `../teasers.csv`, `../baselines.md`).

| Open | What it is |
|---|---|
| `findings.md` | The eight things nearly all the lab films do, where their seconds go (demo, text, logo), the 298-transition mix with median lengths, twelve exemplar moves, nine rules for a 30 s film. |
| `technique-cards.md` | 92 technique cards, grouped by role (hooks, text, transitions, inserts, proof, endings, sound), each with when to use it, a measured recipe and its source film. |
| `directions.md` | Ten directions (D1–D10) clustered from the cards, each with its rules, its films and its risks. |
| `boards/index.html` | The research boards, offline, with a real frame on every card: the reference overview, the five technique collections, the ten directions (`refs.html`); where the seconds go, the transition study with 12 moves as 13-frame strips, and the rules (`motion.html`). |
| `teaser-study.html` | 25 pre-launch teasers, 10 broken down frame by frame, the reveal ladder (how much each one shows, and when the words arrive). |

Each card and direction ends with a "For Kallo" line or a worked-example note: that is how the generic technique was applied to one product. Read it as an example, not as the rule for yours.

**The frames** on the boards and in the teaser study are stills from other companies' public films, credited to film and timestamp, kept as research commentary. They belong to their owners: do not reuse them as imagery, and keep your own research boards with third-party frames private (`../../references/distilling.md` §4).

The corpus is one taste: high-fidelity frontier-lab product films (OpenAI, Claude, xAI, Manus, plus Google for motion) and brand teasers (Samsung, Notion, Nothing, Raycast, Google, Framer, CMF, teenage engineering). For another style, the method transfers and the numbers may not; see `../README.md` for coverage.
