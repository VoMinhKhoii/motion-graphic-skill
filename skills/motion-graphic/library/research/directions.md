# Ten directions

The 92 technique cards ([technique-cards.md](technique-cards.md)) were clustered into ten directions. A direction is a whole visual grammar: a ground, a type system, a camera rule, a transition motif and a sound rule that tend to appear together in the same films. Each one names the films that use it and the cards to build it from.

The "For Kallo" paragraphs are the proposals as they were shown to the owner on 30 September, before any film concept existed. View counts are the sums over the listed films at download time.

How the directions were used next: the five film concepts in [../02-concepts/film-directions.md](../../examples/kallo-launch-film/02-concepts/film-directions.md) each combine one or two directions. The chosen concept, K3 "One day", draws mainly on D9 (light tells the time) and D5 (a device in a colour world). The final film later swapped the gradient world for generated rooms (see [../03-iterations.md](../../examples/kallo-launch-film/03-iterations.md)).

## D1 · The sentence is the interface

21 films, 179M views in total on the source posts. The film is typed. A bold line on a white field is the product’s own input; one rebuilt component at a time steps out of it at hero scale; a dot carries every cut.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- White or off-white field (#FFFFFF, #F7F7F7), sometimes a faint dot grid. Colour lives only in one variable word and in the UI.
- Bold grotesk at 5–9% cap height, centred, typed at 20–34 characters/s straight into its final layout. The product word cycles colour, then settles.
- One rebuilt component at a time (the composer, a toggle, a chip, a bar), cropped to 12–65% of the frame. Never a whole screen.
- One continuous 2D camera: 0–6 cuts a minute. Every move is a fast ease-out, then a 2–4 s hold.
- A dot or a hairline bridges every chapter. The film ends dot → wordmark.
- Sound: ticks under the typing, the bed arrives with the first UI, a dip under the key result.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** Kallo’s input is a sentence, so the film’s typography is the product. Frame 1 types “rice with a grilled chicken thigh, skin removed”; the food words snap into chips; the line collapses into a tan dot that opens on 548 kcal; the same meal then steps out on the iPhone and on the web as single components.

**Fit:** The closest fit: Kallo is literally text in, numbers out.

**Risk:** The most-used grammar in AI launches. Without an owned motif (Be Vietnam Pro, the tan, the macro colours, the ring) it reads as an OpenAI template.

**Cards to build it from:** **H1** Frame 1 is typing; **T1** One variable word cycles through colour; **T4** The colour sweep is the parse; **T6** Typing dots stand in for a word; **P1** The camera rides the caret, then pulls back; **P2** Typed words snap into chips; **P3** One component at hero scale, off the edge; **X1** Collapse to a dot; the dot carries the cut; **X3** A line carries the cut; **X11** The underline becomes a shelf; **E1** Dot → mark → wordmark types on; hold in silence; **E3** The wordmark backspaces into the logo.

**Key frames on the original board:** *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:20.6; *Computer History* (OpenAI) at 0:01.7; *Sites in Codex* (OpenAI) at 0:05.2; *ChatGPT for Financial Services* (OpenAI) at 0:12.3; *Manus Browser Operator* (Manus) at 0:03.4; *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:25.5.

## D2 · Poster-scale UI on a paper world

14 films, 283M views in total on the source posts. Real UI recomposed like a poster: one card or row fills half the frame on a warm paper ground or a patterned pastel wallpaper, driven by a big cursor, with short lines of type between.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- Warm flat grounds (#E2DACD, #F4F4EC), or pastel grounds with a white hand-drawn wave pattern (mint #BDD5C8, lavender #CCCAD8).
- One control or card at 40–81% of the width, cropped hard by the frame. An oversized cursor (14–17% of height) drives each beat.
- Lines fade in word by word (0.08 s stagger) at 5.5–7.4% of height, centred.
- One full-bleed brand-colour card, for the hook or for the turn.
- Few cuts; the camera zooms through the UI. Near-silence until the first result.
- End: dot → spark → the wordmark types on, held in silence.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** Kallo’s cream and tan already live here. Swap the serif for Be Vietnam Pro, terracotta for tan, and the wave for a pattern only food would have (a tablecloth weave, a dot grid). The meal input becomes a poster-scale card on cream, one row at a time.

**Fit:** The warmest fit with Kallo’s palette, and honest product footage.

**Risk:** Cream, warm and one accent is Anthropic’s signature. Kallo on cream will read as a Claude film unless the type and the pattern are clearly Kallo’s.

**Cards to build it from:** **H3** One control, one state change, two seconds; **C6** The problem as clutter; the question in the one gap; **C9** One full-bleed colour card for the turn; **P3** One component at hero scale, off the edge; **P4** The prompt alone, full frame; **P9** A colour sweep rewrites the numbers; **P11** The work as rows that append; **T2** Word-by-word opacity cascade; **T12** The wait as a designed beat; **I3** Patterned paper grounds; **S1** Near-silence until the first result; **E1** Dot → mark → wordmark types on; hold in silence.

**Key frames on the original board:** *Claude Design* (Claude) at 0:00.5; *Salesforce* (Claude) at 0:11.5; *Claude Managed Agents* (Claude) at 0:08.5; *Claude for Office* (Claude) at 0:56.5; *Connectors: Fusion* (Claude) at 0:01.2; *Artifacts in Claude Code* (Claude) at 0:46.2.

## D3 · Specimen assembly

3 films, 94M views in total on the source posts. Real things, scanned flat and packed edge to edge into one big figure. Each shot is a different collection on its own ground; the cuts accelerate, stop dead, and the name lands.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- 10–30 real specimens (scans or top-down photos), flat light, no shadows, touching or overlapping into one figure 70–75% of the frame height.
- One collection and one ground colour per shot (kraft, sage, navy, yellow), with grain and paper texture.
- Stop-motion on twos; pieces land with a slight rotation.
- Cuts accelerate from 1 s to 0.125 s over ~12 s, then a 0.75 s hold in silence.
- The name in large type (8–13% of height) on the same ground, held 3 s.
- Empty ground only around the figure, never between the pieces. The sparse state lasts under 1 s.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** Build the number from the meal. The rice, chicken and greens of “rice with a grilled chicken thigh, skin removed” pack into “548”; then a ramen bowl’s parts into its total; then a burrito’s. A different kitchen per shot, cutting faster, stopping on one, which resolves into the app card.

**Fit:** Turns “numbers from real food” into a single image, and photoreal top-down food is something we can make.

**Risk:** Needs 15–30 consistent top-down cut-outs per figure. It is Claude’s model-teaser grammar, so the figure must be ours (the number, the ring), and never a lone item on empty ground.

**Cards to build it from:** **H5** A crowded frame 1; one thing flies at the lens; **I1** Build the form from a dense collection; **R1** Accelerate, then stop dead; **S4** A riser into a hard stop; **X8** One thing flies into the lens; **S7** Built for muted autoplay.

**Key frames on the original board:** *Claude Opus 5* (Claude) at 0:04.5; *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:11.2; *Claude Sonnet 5* (Claude) at 0:01.0; *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:00.1; *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:12.3; *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:16.1.

## D4 · Macro material

4 films, 43M views in total on the source posts. Extreme close-ups of one material fill the frame; one contour stays in the same place across the cuts; a single line of type rests on its edge; the name lands in silence.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- The subject fills 45–100% of the frame; its edge is a curve with the apex at 45–55% of height.
- Negative space is black or one colour; rim light on the edge; grain.
- One saturated hue per shot, or the material’s real colour.
- Cuts land on the music grid (~0.5 s), doubling in the second half; or three shots of 0.8–1.1 s.
- One line (7–8.5% of height) blurs in on the edge; the words grow shot by shot.
- The music drops out for the name.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** Food at macro scale (a rice grain, a crust, a yolk, broth), where the contour is Kallo’s own: the ring gauge’s 270° arc. The arc travels from food edges into the app’s ring, which then fills with the real macros.

**Fit:** The most premium look; food texture is tactile and appetising.

**Risk:** The owner asked for no direct copy of the Opus 5.5 horizon cuts. The motif has to be Kallo’s ring, not a horizon, and the macro food photography must be excellent or it looks like stock.

**Cards to build it from:** **H4** Start too close to read; **C2** The subject fills the lower half; the line sits on its edge; **R2** Busy first, still for the message; **S5** Silence for the name; one sound for the mark; **E6** The end loops to frame 1.

**Key frames on the original board:** *Claude Opus 5.5* (Claude) at 0:06.2; *Claude Opus 5.5* (Claude) at 0:13.1; *DevDay sting* (OpenAI) at 0:01.2; *dots* (OpenAI) at 0:02.8; *Rename sting* (SpaceXAI) at 0:02.5; *dots* (OpenAI) at 0:20.3.

## D5 · A device slab in a colour world

10 films, 65M views in total on the source posts. The phone is a flat, front-on slab with no hardware, set in a soft world (a gradient, a sky, paper, a painting). The camera pushes inside until one card fills the frame; the web is a window in the same world.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- Phone: a white or cream slab with a 9–24 px frosted rim, no buttons, at 80–87% of height or cropped by the bottom edge. Taps are translucent dots.
- World: a blurred gradient, a sky, a painted plate, a wireframe room; one colour family per film.
- Push in ~2.3× in 0.5 s until one card fills 57–64% of the width. Chapters cut on 2.5 s title cards.
- Words flank the phone (2–3 words left and right) instead of captions over the UI.
- One outline can morph between devices: portrait to landscape in 0.42 s.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** The honest way to show iOS and the web together: the iPhone as a cream slab on the brand sweep; its outline widens into the web window as “on your iPhone” becomes “on the web”, with the same meal on both.

**Fit:** Shows both platforms truthfully with real UI; no hands, no renders.

**Risk:** On its own it is a product demo, not a launch idea. Pair it with D1 or D3.

**Cards to build it from:** **P6** The device is a flat slab in a colour world; **P7** A floating window; push past its edge; **X4** One outline morphs into the next device; **C7** Two devices, one action, the focus swaps; **X2** Zoom-through into its place in the product; **H2** The headline turns out to be the app.

## D6 · The dark instrument

9 films, 414M views in total on the source posts. A black stage where the product reads like an instrument: idle dots turn into live bars, grey words snap to white, there is one accent colour, and the product’s own sound is the soundtrack.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- Near-black ground (#080808), one accent (orange #EB5811), rebuilt dark cards (#161616 with 1 px #2A2A2A borders).
- Everything sits in the middle half; spaced-caps labels; one centred line at a time.
- Idle = a grey dotted line. Live = accent bars or a karaoke caption.
- No music: silence gates every event, and the picture only moves while the product makes sound.
- Variant: a grain gradient world with letter-by-letter blur resolves and a steady tick pulse.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** A night version of logging: three macro slots sit idle as dotted lines and each goes live as the sentence is parsed, while the meal line snaps from grey to ink. Every sound is caused by the product.

**Fit:** Precise, credible, and reads perfectly on mute; strong for a “how it works” cut.

**Risk:** Cold for food, and Kallo’s palette is warm. Better as a chapter or a cut-down than the whole film.

**Cards to build it from:** **C5** Instrument layout: four rows in the middle half; **H7** Give the viewer a question; **T4** The colour sweep is the parse; **T3** Blur to sharp, letter by letter; **P12** The pipeline lights up in turn; **S2** The product is the soundtrack; **I2** A soft colour world instead of a void.

## D7 · A cast plays the product

8 films, 50M views in total on the source posts. Brand primitives become characters (discs with glyph eyes, pixel sprites, clay creatures, trading cards) and act out one simple rule: a game, a race, a select screen.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- Characters are built from the brand’s own shapes and colours: flat saturated discs or low-res sprites.
- One rule the viewer gets instantly (a maze, a race with timers, three cards side by side).
- Stepped, low frame-rate animation for sprites; springy pops for discs.
- Sound effects only: one blip per action, one bigger hit for the payoff.
- The type builds out of the cast (dots fly to the anchor points of the letters).

**For Kallo (as proposed):** Kallo’s bun and the ingredients as a cast: a race between a food-database search and one typed sentence, or the day’s protein as a dot maze that the Kallo mark clears.

**Fit:** The most memorable and the most loopable; a strong teaser before the hero film.

**Risk:** A toy tone, and the features read less. A teaser or cut-down, not the launch film itself.

**Cards to build it from:** **H6** The whole system is on screen at frame 1; **C4** Side by side, the same task; **T8** Letters built from anchor points; **E7** The reveal as a physical event; **S2** The product is the soundtrack.

## D8 · A drawn idea hands over to the UI

4 films, 31M views in total on the source posts. A hand-drawn sketch (a boiling marker line, paper cut-outs) states the idea, and its drawn screen or plate becomes the real UI at the same size and in the same place.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- Black marker ~1.7% of height that “boils” (redrawn every 2 frames), on warm grey or cream.
- Stepped timing (~16 drawings/s) and one terracotta accent.
- The drawing’s area fills and becomes the real UI at the same position and size.
- Later, marker scribbles point at exactly what an action changes.
- The invisible work can be a halftone cloud with a status pill.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** A one-line marker plate with a question mark: the plate fills and becomes the result card. Or a hand sets down one dot (the meal) that splits into its ingredients.

**Fit:** Warm and human, and the clearest way to explain “we break your meal into ingredients”.

**Risk:** It needs real craft (the boil, the stepped timing) or it looks like clip art.

**Cards to build it from:** **I8** A drawn sketch hands over to the UI; **I9** A hand mark over crisp UI; **I7** The invisible work as a texture; **X4** One outline morphs into the next device.

**Key frames on the original board:** *Claude Security* (Claude) at 0:04.8; *Work tools on mobile* (Claude) at 0:03.9; *Financial agent templates* (Claude) at 0:01.5; *Cowork* (Claude) at 0:30.0; *Claude connector for Blender* (Claude) at 0:43.2; *Recommended Connectors* (Manus) at 0:01.8.

## D9 · Light tells the time

4 films, 13M views in total on the source posts. Light is the story: night turns to dawn behind the product, a sun sits on a horizon, a moon drifts behind the headline. Time passes without a cut.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- One continuous shot where the ground itself changes (navy #212838 → cream #FAF2E5 in 1.3 s).
- Flat night skies (stars of 2–6 px, a flat moon disc with a soft glow) and a rising sun disc.
- The product card restyles as its clock advances.
- Bold rounded sans at 6–7% cap height, one headline per phase.
- The music drops out before the logo.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** A day of eating in 20 s: “8:12 AM oats” under a small sun on cream, “12:40 PM chicken rice”, “7:05 PM pasta” under the moon, the day’s total climbing, the iPhone at breakfast and the web at night.

**Fit:** A whole world with no people and no empty cream; it says “every meal, all day” naturally.

**Risk:** Few references (one film carries most of it), and the light story can slow the pace.

**Cards to build it from:** **X10** Light tells the time; **P16** One day, told with the clock; **R6** The turn sits at 70–85% of the runtime.

**Key frames on the original board:** *Website Auto Publish* (Manus) at 0:20.6; *Website Auto Publish* (Manus) at 0:16.8; *Daybreak* (OpenAI) at 0:05.6; *Daybreak expansion* (OpenAI) at 0:02.0; *GPT-5.6 Luna for everyone* (OpenAI) at 0:06.9; *Website Auto Publish* (Manus) at 0:19.6.

## D10 · Living posters (the companion posts)

11 films, 32M views in total on the source posts. Not the launch film: the posts around it. A static layout where one thing moves, looping silently in the feed.

**Rules (measured from the films):**

- Seamless loops of 6–19.23 s where the first and last frames match, often with no audio stream.
- One template per partner or platform; only the tint and one word change.
- A lockup over a grain fluid gradient, or a headline with a UI card bleeding off two edges.
- One moving element: a toggle, a caret, a sprite, a drifting moon.

**For Kallo (as proposed):** “Now on the web”, “Now in Japanese”, a square with the result card bleeding off a corner, “Guess the protein” with empty slots: one template, many posts.

**Fit:** Cheap and repeatable; keeps launch week going.

**Risk:** A supplement to the film, not a replacement.

**Cards to build it from:** **E8** Cut-downs: the living poster; **S7** Built for muted autoplay; **C9** One full-bleed colour card for the turn.
