# Technique cards

All 92 technique cards from the Kallo reference study, as text. Each card is one technique that the study saw in at least one of the 85 motion-design launch films (see [corpus.md](../../examples/kallo-launch-film/01-research/corpus.md)) and measured from real frames. Duplicates found by different study passes were merged into one card.

How to read a card:
- **Code and name.** The letter is the role: H hooks, C composition, T type, I imagery, P product, X transitions, R rhythm, S sound, E endings.
- **When to use.** The job the technique does.
- **Recipe.** The measured numbers. Sizes are a share of the frame (cap height as % of frame height, widths as % of frame width). Timings come from 8 to 12 fps frame bursts. Colours were sampled from pixels.
- **For Kallo.** How the card was proposed for this film at the time. Many of these were never used; they are kept to show how a card turns into a concrete idea.
- **Source.** The first film the card was proven on, and the timestamp of the proving frame. The original cards showed that frame next to the recipe. This copy names the film and the timestamp only, so you can find the frame yourself.

The cards say "SpaceXAI" because that is the account the xAI films were downloaded from.

## 1. Hooks and composition

How the first three seconds and each frame are built. 20 cards, each merged from the techniques the eight study passes recorded.

### Hooks · the first 3 seconds

#### H1 · Frame 1 is typing

- **When to use:** Naming the product with its own gesture.
- **Recipe:** One centred line, cap height 5–8% of the frame, types at 20–34 characters/s straight into its final layout (no reflow). Fresh letters land in an accent and settle to ink, or the product word flickers through 4–8 colours every 0.125 s and settles by 1.5 s.
- **For Kallo:** The meal types itself on frame 1: “rice with a grilled chicken thigh, skin removed” at ~20 characters/s, food words landing tan, then settling to ink.
- **Source:** *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:00.6. Also seen in: *Manus Browser Operator* (Manus) at 0:01.6; *Codex rate limits* (OpenAI) at 0:00.4; *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:00.9; *ChatGPT Work* (OpenAI) at 0:04.9.

#### H2 · The headline turns out to be the app

- **When to use:** Naming it and showing it in one beat, no cut.
- **Recipe:** A two-line centred line (7.1% of height per line) holds on cream for 1.1 s. At 2.0–2.25 s a frameless phone slab (45% of width) rises from below around the words, which never move: they were the app’s screen all along.
- **For Kallo:** The typed meal sits alone on the ground; at 2 s the iPhone slab rises around it and the words become Kallo’s input.
- **Source:** *Dispatch* (Claude) at 0:02.2.

#### H3 · One control, one state change, two seconds

- **When to use:** A muted 2 s hook with a single interaction.
- **Recipe:** A single component (49–59% of width) alone on off-white or full-bleed brand colour. A cursor glides in (0–0.4 s, ease-out) or the card pushes in 5%. The state flips in 2 frames at ~1.2 s; hold to 2.25 s; hard cut.
- **For Kallo:** One ingredient row on tan: “Chicken thigh, skin removed · — kcal” flips to its number in 2 frames. That flip is the hook.
- **Source:** *Connectors: Fusion* (Claude) at 0:01.2. Also seen in: *Claude Design* (Claude) at 0:00.5; *Claude connector for Blender* (Claude) at 0:01.2; *Manus Skills* (Manus) at 0:01.9.

#### H4 · Start too close to read

- **When to use:** Stopping the scroll with abstraction first.
- **Recipe:** Frame 1 is 100% one surface or shape (a disc, fur, the crop of a mark), unreadable. Pull out in 0.9 s ease-out until it reads, or hold 0.8–1.1 s per macro shot with a slow push, then cut.
- **For Kallo:** Open inside one digit of the result, or the grain of the rice, edge to edge, then pull out in 0.9 s to the full line.
- **Source:** *DevDay teaser* (OpenAI) at 0:00.1. Also seen in: *DevDay sting* (OpenAI) at 0:01.2; *Rename sting* (SpaceXAI) at 0:02.5.

#### H5 · A crowded frame 1; one thing flies at the lens

- **When to use:** Scroll-stop in the first 3 frames.
- **Recipe:** The first frame is a full-bleed archival plate crowded with 8+ specimens, zero empty space. One cut-out (~45% of width) is already mid-flight toward camera with ~20 px motion blur; the next cut lands at 0.125 s.
- **For Kallo:** Frame 1 is a full table of everyday dishes from many kitchens, top-down, edge to edge; one lime wedge flies at the lens. Never one item on empty cream.
- **Source:** *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:00.1.

#### H6 · The whole system is on screen at frame 1

- **When to use:** Stating the rule before anything moves.
- **Recipe:** A grid of dots already fills the frame (20 px dots, 96 px pitch at 1080p) with a HUD bar top-right; or 3 dots multiply into a 2×5 matrix by 0.5 s with the title split above and below it.
- **For Kallo:** Frame 1: one dot per ingredient in the sentence; each dot later opens into that ingredient’s row.
- **Source:** *Livestream teaser* (OpenAI) at 0:01.6. Also seen in: *Mathematical Advances* (OpenAI) at 0:01.5.

#### H7 · Give the viewer a question

- **When to use:** A task before the product appears.
- **Recipe:** Digital silence 0–2 s. A question fades in at 39% of height (4.7% block, 47% of width), then at 1.3–1.8 s rises to 24% and shrinks to 84% while empty answer slots (grey dotted lines) fade in below.
- **For Kallo:** “How much protein is in this?” rises into a header; three empty macro slots wait below; then the meal sentence types in.
- **Source:** *Voice cloning quiz* (SpaceXAI) at 0:01.6.

#### H8 · The logo rewrites itself into the message

- **When to use:** Turning a logo opener into a line in 2 s.
- **Recipe:** The lockup holds 1 s (each logo ~4% of height). From 1.15 s words roll up from below one every 0.125 s, each rising one line height in 0.25 s as the logo rolls out. Or the mark assembles stroke by stroke in grey (0.12–0.75 s), then brightens over 0.5 s.
- **For Kallo:** The Kallo wordmark rolls out word by word into “Kallo just read your lunch.” in under 2 s.
- **Source:** *Similarweb upgrade* (Manus) at 0:01.4. Also seen in: *Rename sting* (SpaceXAI) at 0:00.6.

#### H9 · Energy lands at 2 s

- **When to use:** Turning a quiet typed opening into momentum.
- **Recipe:** Solid colour blocks slam in on the beat at 2.3, 2.8 and 3.3 s, one word each (x-height 15% of the frame), hard cut-in with no easing, bleeding off the edges and overlapping.
- **For Kallo:** “type” / “what” / “you ate” on three warm blocks at 2 s, one per beat, over cropped Kallo result cards.
- **Source:** *Sites in Codex* (OpenAI) at 0:03.4.

#### H10 · Frame 0 is a designed poster

- **When to use:** Controlling the paused preview on X and Threads.
- **Recipe:** Frames 0–2 (0.1 s) are a separate thumbnail: the headline at ~7% cap top-centre over the product UI cropped by the bottom edge. The film proper starts on frame 3.
- **For Kallo:** Frame 0: the typed meal over its result card, cropped by the bottom edge, so the muted preview already explains Kallo.
- **Source:** *PowerPoint mode* (Manus) at 0:00.0.

### Composition · how a frame is built

#### C1 · Edges full, centre clear

- **When to use:** A headline among many examples; the opposite of a void.
- **Recipe:** The headline sits in a clear band ~22% of height at the centre. 4–8 product crops, each 35–45% of width, bleed 30–40% off the corners and edges at mixed scales. The frame is full; only the reading line has air.
- **For Kallo:** “Any meal, counted.” in the middle; cropped Kallo result cards (ramen, a burrito bowl, eggs on toast, a salad) bleed off all four corners.
- **Source:** *Sites in Codex* (OpenAI) at 0:05.2. Also seen in: *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 1:14.1; *ChatGPT for Financial Services* (OpenAI) at 1:16.0; *PowerPoint mode* (Manus) at 0:15.3.

#### C2 · The subject fills the lower half; the line sits on its edge

- **When to use:** Mood and one claim per shot.
- **Recipe:** A convex surface fills the lower 45–65% of the frame, apex at 45–55% of height, plain dark or single-colour space above. One line (7% cap) sits on or straddles the edge; blurs in over 0.5 s, holds ~1.8 s.
- **For Kallo:** Use Kallo’s own curve, the ring gauge’s arc or a bowl’s rim, with one line resting on it. Borrow the framing, not the Opus 5.5 horizon montage.
- **Source:** *Claude Opus 5.5* (Claude) at 0:06.2. Also seen in: *dots* (OpenAI) at 0:02.8; *Daybreak expansion* (OpenAI) at 0:02.0.

#### C3 · One frame, three cells: in, work, out

- **When to use:** A claim with an input and an output.
- **Recipe:** 1 px #D8D8D8 hairlines at 39% and 60% of width and 31% and 69% of height; the sentence splits across cells, flush to the hairlines; the bottom row fades to white.
- **For Kallo:** Left “rice with a grilled chicken thigh, skin removed”, middle the ingredients resolving, right “548 kcal · 37 g protein”. Text in, numbers out, in one still.
- **Source:** *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:06.5.

#### C4 · Side by side, the same task

- **When to use:** Speed or difference without a claim.
- **Recipe:** Two or three portrait cards (31–47% of width, 93–96% of height, 16 px radius, 1 px border) run the same thing: live timers, idle vs live states, or a stat block each. Only the contents move; the layout never does.
- **For Kallo:** Left: a food-database search, its timer running while fields fill. Right: Kallo, one sentence, the macros land, timer frozen. Real timings only.
- **Source:** *Ultrafast* (OpenAI) at 0:20.0. Also seen in: *Voice cloning API* (SpaceXAI) at 0:14.5; *Branch* (Manus) at 0:08.3; *Sol and Luna cards* (OpenAI) at 0:02.5.

#### C5 · Instrument layout: four rows in the middle half

- **When to use:** A question, the slots, the caption; nothing else.
- **Recipe:** Everything inside 25–75% of width. Rows at 23.5% (question), 43.3% (slots), 57.5% (labels), 71.9% (caption) of height; the top 21% and bottom 26% stay empty. Two symmetric columns; no logo, rule or border.
- **For Kallo:** Question at the top, three macro slots (PROTEIN · CARBS · FAT) in a row, the typed meal as the caption.
- **Source:** *Voice cloning quiz* (SpaceXAI) at 0:16.0.

#### C6 · The problem as clutter; the question in the one gap

- **When to use:** Stating the pain before the product.
- **Recipe:** 12–16 overlapping windows and notes slide in from the edges over 13 s until they cover ~70% of the frame, leaving one horizontal gap ~25% of height where the question sits. Then everything scatters off-frame to one line.
- **For Kallo:** A pile of the old way (a nutrition label, a gram spreadsheet, a scale readout, five calorie-site tabs); in the gap: “Logging one meal shouldn’t take five tabs.”
- **Source:** *Claude Managed Agents* (Claude) at 0:08.5.

#### C7 · Two devices, one action, the focus swaps

- **When to use:** One action on the phone, work on another screen.
- **Recipe:** Phone slab 12.5% of width at x 6–19%; laptop silhouette from 39% of width bleeding off the right. The idle device is defocused (~6 px); focus jumps to whichever device is acting; a hand-drawn line joins them.
- **For Kallo:** The iPhone types “two eggs, toast, half an avocado”; the web dashboard on the right fills the day’s rings. iOS and web in one frame, one account.
- **Source:** *Dispatch* (Claude) at 0:12.5.

#### C8 · Breadth as an orbit

- **When to use:** Many sources or integrations, no list.
- **Recipe:** ~34 tiles (9% of height) stream in along an ellipse (96% × 79% of the frame) around a centred headline, closing the ring in ~2 s; the ring turns for 3.5 s, then collapses into the centre.
- **For Kallo:** “Real food data” ringed by ingredient chips (“brown rice, 150 g”, “olive oil, 1 tbsp”); they collapse into one result card.
- **Source:** *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:47.0. Also seen in: *Cloud Computer* (Manus) at 0:32.0.

#### C9 · One full-bleed colour card for the turn

- **When to use:** The single line that reframes the argument.
- **Recipe:** After 18 s on cream, a hard cut to full-bleed terracotta holding one two-line centred line (5.7% per line), words fading in one by one; 4 s, then back.
- **For Kallo:** Stay on cream all film and switch to tan once, for “No scanning. No searching. Just say it.”
- **Source:** *Financial agent templates* (Claude) at 0:19.5.

#### C10 · Pinned headline; the content steps sideways

- **When to use:** Several examples without new title cards.
- **Recipe:** The headline is pinned top-centre (y ~11%, cap ~7%) for 13 s. Centre window 61% × 70%; neighbours at 0.75 scale and 50% opacity, cropped by the edges; steps sideways in ~0.6 s ease-in-out.
- **For Kallo:** “Any meal. Any kitchen.” pinned; phone and web windows step sideways, each with a different meal typed and resolved.
- **Source:** *Manus Skills* (Manus) at 0:16.0.

## 2. Type and imagery

How words move, and what the world is made of. 23 cards, each merged from the techniques the eight study passes recorded.

### Type

#### T1 · One variable word cycles through colour

- **When to use:** Saying “any X” in one line.
- **Recipe:** Fixed sentence, bold grotesk ~7–8% cap. The last word swaps every 0.17–0.75 s, each in its own colour; the line re-centres on each swap and settles on the last after ~1.5 s.
- **For Kallo:** “Kallo counts your [breakfast / ramen / leftovers / 2 am toast]”, each in a macro colour, landing in ink.
- **Source:** *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:11.9. Also seen in: *ChatGPT for Financial Services* (OpenAI) at 0:02.4; *ChatGPT Work* (OpenAI) at 0:04.9.

#### T2 · Word-by-word opacity cascade

- **When to use:** Every line, when there is no voiceover.
- **Recipe:** Words appear in place with no movement: 35% → 100% opacity over 0.25 s, starting 0.08–0.12 s apart, so 5–6 words resolve in ~0.6 s; hold ~2.3 s. Manus variant: each word enters 6–8 px low at 60% grey.
- **For Kallo:** Every Kallo line (“Say what you ate.”): 4–6 words, 0.1 s stagger, one soft tick per word.
- **Source:** *Connectors: Fusion* (Claude) at 0:02.6. Also seen in: *Claude connector for Blender* (Claude) at 0:02.8; *PowerPoint mode* (Manus) at 0:09.0.

#### T3 · Blur to sharp, letter by letter

- **When to use:** Introducing each capability as one short phrase.
- **Recipe:** Each letter starts at ~12 px blur and 0% opacity, 0.04 s apart, sharpening over 0.3 s; the phrase settles in 0.5–0.6 s while letter-spacing eases from +40% to 0.
- **For Kallo:** Each parsed ingredient name resolves this way as Kallo reads the sentence; then the kcal number does the same.
- **Source:** *Grok Build* (SpaceXAI) at 0:18.6. Also seen in: *Grok Build* (SpaceXAI) at 0:00.2.

#### T4 · The colour sweep is the parse

- **When to use:** Showing progress through a sentence.
- **Recipe:** The line appears whole in grey (#4A4A4A); each word snaps to white in ~0.08 s (5 frames at 60 fps), no slide or scale, in step with the product. Variant: a gradient band travels through the letters at half a frame width per second.
- **For Kallo:** “rice with a grilled chicken thigh, skin removed” appears whole in grey; each phrase snaps to ink as Kallo recognises it.
- **Source:** *Voice cloning quiz* (SpaceXAI) at 0:13.0. Also seen in: *GPT-5.6 Luna for everyone* (OpenAI) at 0:06.9; *Similarweb data* (Manus) at 0:01.5.

#### T5 · Highlight what the product picks out

- **When to use:** Plain language, with its key parts marked.
- **Recipe:** Inside the prompt at ~10.5% cap height, lines cropped by the edges. After a key phrase finishes typing, a rounded highlight wipes under it in ~0.375 s. Variant: camera at 5× with depth of field; the key phrase types in the accent.
- **For Kallo:** Tan highlights land under “rice”, “grilled chicken thigh” and “skin removed”; the highlights become the ingredient rows in the next shot.
- **Source:** *Cowork* (Claude) at 0:06.5. Also seen in: *Voice Agent Builder* (SpaceXAI) at 0:12.2.

#### T6 · Typing dots stand in for a word

- **When to use:** The thinking beat, inside a sentence.
- **Recipe:** “Your” (8.3% cap), then dots (4.8% of height, 74 px pitch) appear one per 0.17 s up to six and bob in a ±35 px wave; after ~1 s they collapse right to left while the real word types in their place.
- **For Kallo:** “Your lunch is ●●●”, then the dots collapse into “548 kcal”. The analysis becomes a pause in the sentence.
- **Source:** *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:58.6.

#### T7 · Draw the empty brackets first

- **When to use:** An ownable input/output type system.
- **Recipe:** Bold grotesk 6.9% cap. An empty violet “[ ]” appears, holds 0.2 s, and spreads as the word types inside at ~15 characters/s; then an empty “( )” filled the same way.
- **For Kallo:** Kallo’s pair: the meal inside quotes, the result after “=”. Draw the empty quotes, type inside, then the number counts in.
- **Source:** *Sites in Codex* (OpenAI) at 0:13.3.

#### T8 · Letters built from anchor points

- **When to use:** Making the message look constructed.
- **Recipe:** ~30 dots (2.2% of height) fly to the bezier anchors of the words in 0.15 s; 4 px outlines draw between them over 0.15 s; the glyphs fill in 0.25 s. Cap height 23%.
- **For Kallo:** Each ingredient becomes a dot that flies to an anchor of “548”; outlines draw; the digits fill. The food becomes the number.
- **Source:** *DevDay teaser* (OpenAI) at 0:05.3.

#### T9 · Decode the value

- **When to use:** Signalling computation.
- **Recipe:** An 8-character string of symbols at 15% opacity re-rolls every 0.125 s; letters lock left to right one per 0.125 s while opacity ramps to 100% by ~0.8 s.
- **For Kallo:** Decode the numbers, not the name: the kcal field re-rolls and locks to “548 kcal”, then “P 37 g”. Under 1.3 s.
- **Source:** *Daybreak* (OpenAI) at 0:05.6.

#### T10 · A giant word drops into its slot

- **When to use:** Connecting a big idea to the real input.
- **Recipe:** The variable word at 11% cap, letters rising with a 70 ms stagger in a warm-to-cool gradient; then it scales down ~3.5× into its place inside the typed prompt, which fades in behind it at 2.2×. Repeats every ~5 s with a new word.
- **For Kallo:** “ramen” / “shakshuka” / “burrito bowl” huge, each dropping into “I had ___” in Kallo’s input as its numbers land.
- **Source:** *Similarweb data* (Manus) at 0:03.5. Also seen in: *Similarweb data* (Manus) at 0:09.6.

#### T11 · A roll of names past a focus band

- **When to use:** Many items in 3–5 s.
- **Recipe:** A column of names at headline size scrolls ~2 rows/s (row pitch 13% of height) beside a fixed phrase; the row level with it is full colour, the rest ~55% grey. Variants: three columns at different speeds with depth of field; a reel decelerating onto the noun.
- **For Kallo:** “pho / tacos al pastor / jollof rice / carbonara / bibimbap … counted by Kallo”: ten dishes from ten kitchens in 5 s.
- **Source:** *Small business collection* (OpenAI) at 0:15.6. Also seen in: *Financial agent templates* (Claude) at 0:12.5; *Voice Agent Builder* (SpaceXAI) at 0:37.0; *Branch* (Manus) at 0:01.5.

#### T12 · The wait as a designed beat

- **When to use:** Turning processing time into a moment.
- **Recipe:** Full frame, no UI: a spark and an italic gerund at ~9.6% cap revealed through a soft left-to-right mask, ~1 s per verb. Or a white pill over the output being built behind it, which then collapses to a dot.
- **For Kallo:** “Finding ingredients…” → “Matching food data…” → “Weighing portions…”, 0.6 s each: the real pipeline, told honestly.
- **Source:** *Claude Security* (Claude) at 0:31.3. Also seen in: *Claude Design* (Claude) at 0:18.0; *Claude Design* (Claude) at 0:09.0.

#### T13 · A line wider than the frame, read by the camera

- **When to use:** Giving one phrase scale and time.
- **Recipe:** Headline at 11% cap, ~1.45 frame widths long; the camera pans at ~half a frame width per second, near-linear; foreground objects exit faster (parallax).
- **For Kallo:** Pan along “rice with a grilled chicken thigh, skin removed” set huge, as if reading it, and land on the number.
- **Source:** *Cloud Computer* (Manus) at 0:02.6.

#### T14 · One line of counts; sources float around it

- **When to use:** Summarising what the product found.
- **Recipe:** The app blurs (~10 px) and fades to off-white; highlights lift out as 6 mono chips (3.5% of height) that drift around one centred line of counts.
- **For Kallo:** “548 kcal · 37 g protein · 13 g fat” as one line; the matched foods drift around it in mono (“chicken thigh, skinless, grilled”).
- **Source:** *Claude for Word* (Claude) at 0:17.0.

### Imagery · what the world is made of

#### I1 · Build the form from a dense collection

- **When to use:** A hero number made of the subject.
- **Recipe:** 10–30 real specimens (scans or top-down photos) touching or overlapping into one figure 70–75% of frame height, flat light, no shadows, one ground colour per collection. Empty space only around the figure, never between pieces; complete by 2.4 s.
- **For Kallo:** Build the kcal number from that meal’s own ingredients, packed edge to edge, a different kitchen per shot. The fix for lone cut-outs.
- **Source:** *Claude Opus 5* (Claude) at 0:04.5. Also seen in: *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:11.2; *Claude Sonnet 5* (Claude) at 0:01.0.

#### I2 · A soft colour world instead of a void

- **When to use:** A minimal film that still feels tactile.
- **Recipe:** A full-bleed mesh gradient of 2–3 soft blobs that drift (one crosses ~30% of width in 2 s), fine film grain, no hard edges; or a heavy blur (radius ~10% of width) of warm colour behind one crisp UI element.
- **For Kallo:** The brand sweep (cream, apricot, lilac) as a drifting grain world behind the input and the numbers. Never flat, empty cream.
- **Source:** *Grok Build* (SpaceXAI) at 0:02.2. Also seen in: *Voice Agent Builder* (SpaceXAI) at 0:30.3; *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:39.5; *Codex on mobile* (OpenAI) at 0:02.6.

#### I3 · Patterned paper grounds

- **When to use:** A house background that is never flat.
- **Recipe:** A light ground with a 2–3 px dot grid (~16 px pitch, ~3% contrast) plus soft darker patches at the corners; or mint or lavender with a white hand-drawn wave or loop pattern.
- **For Kallo:** Cream with a faint tan dot grid and denser corner patches, or a woven tablecloth pattern: a texture only Kallo uses.
- **Source:** *Manus Skills* (Manus) at 0:04.5. Also seen in: *Scheduled Tasks 2.0* (Manus) at 0:01.2; *Claude Code desktop* (Claude) at 0:01.0.

#### I4 · Real UI on a painted ground

- **When to use:** Warm, authored product shots.
- **Recipe:** A full-bleed impasto painting (visible brush ridges and canvas weave) behind a frameless UI panel (47–61% of width) that casts a soft shadow onto it and is cropped by the bottom edge.
- **For Kallo:** One painted still life (a market table, a tablecloth) as the only ground behind Kallo’s screens; the landing page’s oil sketches already fit.
- **Source:** *Projects on mobile* (Manus) at 0:03.0. Also seen in: *GPT Image 2 in Slides* (Manus) at 0:04.8.

#### I5 · Exploded view

- **When to use:** Showing what something is made of.
- **Recipe:** The output alone, full frame on white, no chrome. An isometric object (52% of width) whose 5 layers drift apart over 1.5 s (near-linear), then step to a wider final spacing.
- **For Kallo:** A real plate lifts apart into floating layers (rice, chicken thigh, the skin set aside, sauce), each with its gram label.
- **Source:** *Connectors: Fusion* (Claude) at 0:36.5.

#### I6 · Tokens carry the data

- **When to use:** Data moving, with no illustration.
- **Recipe:** ~17 small squares (1.5% of width, four greys) fly in from the edges trailing 1 px hairlines (8–15% of width), easing out near the text over 1.5 s; later they stack on hairline stems into a chart with no axes.
- **For Kallo:** Tan tokens fly into the typed meal, gather under each food word, then stack into protein, carbs and fat stems at their gram heights.
- **Source:** *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:02.3. Also seen in: *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:10.5.

#### I7 · The invisible work as a texture

- **When to use:** A process with no natural picture.
- **Recipe:** A square dot grid (18 px pitch) whose dot size follows a soft radial blob, with one white status pill at the centre; or raw data characters piling into a wave that fills the lower 60%, then resolving into the finished page.
- **For Kallo:** The lookup as a short tan halftone cloud (0.8–1.2 s) with a “Matching food data” pill, condensing into the four numbers.
- **Source:** *Cowork* (Claude) at 0:30.0. Also seen in: *Artifacts in Claude Code* (Claude) at 0:12.0; *Daybreak expansion* (OpenAI) at 0:02.0.

#### I8 · A drawn sketch hands over to the UI

- **When to use:** Idea first, product second.
- **Recipe:** Black marker (~1.7% of height) that “boils” (redrawn every 2 frames) on warm grey; the drawing’s screen or plate area fills and becomes the real UI at the same position and size. Or paper shapes collapse into a blob that squares off into the phone.
- **For Kallo:** A one-line marker plate with a question mark; the line erases and the plate becomes Kallo’s result card at the same size.
- **Source:** *Claude Security* (Claude) at 0:04.8. Also seen in: *Work tools on mobile* (Claude) at 0:03.9; *Financial agent templates* (Claude) at 0:01.5.

#### I9 · A hand mark over crisp UI

- **When to use:** Pointing at exactly what an action affects.
- **Recipe:** A terracotta marker scribble (~2.2% of height, ~6 passes) drawn in 0.5 s across the rows an action affects, then cleared; or thin 1–2 px pen sparkles and a dashed arc around a card the moment it helps.
- **For Kallo:** A tan strike through “skin” when “skin removed” applies; a thin sparkle at the card when the numbers resolve.
- **Source:** *Claude connector for Blender* (Claude) at 0:43.2. Also seen in: *Recommended Connectors* (Manus) at 0:01.8; *Projects on mobile* (Manus) at 0:07.0.

## 3. Product

How the UI appears: rebuilt, cropped, pushed into. 16 cards, each merged from the techniques the eight study passes recorded.

### Product · input

#### P1 · The camera rides the caret, then pulls back

- **When to use:** Making the prompt readable on a phone feed.

## 4. Transitions and rhythm

How one shot becomes the next, and how time is spent. 18 cards, each merged from the techniques the eight study passes recorded.

### Transitions

#### X1 · Collapse to a dot; the dot carries the cut

- **When to use:** Chapter to chapter with one object.
- **Recipe:** A line shrinks to a dot in ~0.2 s; the dot swaps colour every 0.17–0.2 s for ~0.8 s; then it grows from the centre to fill the frame in ~0.4 s (ease-in, exponential) and the next line types inside. Or the dot seeds the next diagram.
- **For Kallo:** The typed meal collapses to one tan dot, cycles through the macro colours, then irises open and “548 kcal” types inside it.
- **Source:** *Astra for Law* (OpenAI) at 0:07.5. Also seen in: *ChatGPT for Financial Services* (OpenAI) at 0:29.5; *Mathematical Advances* (OpenAI) at 0:08.1; *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:36.8.

#### X2 · Zoom-through into its place in the product

- **When to use:** From the big idea to where it lives.
- **Recipe:** A full-frame element (a 64%-wide bubble, a giant word, a lifted logo token 18% of height) scales down 2.3–3.5× in 0.35–0.8 s while the UI fades in behind at the matching crop, so it lands exactly in its slot.
- **For Kallo:** The full-frame meal sentence shrinks into Kallo’s input on the iPhone, the result card already filling in below.
- **Source:** *Dispatch* (Claude) at 0:08.9. Also seen in: *Similarweb data* (Manus) at 0:09.6; *Shopify connector* (Manus) at 0:03.0.

#### X3 · A line carries the cut

- **When to use:** Turning a sentence into a diagram.
- **Recipe:** Deleted words leave a 1 px #D1D1D1 hairline joining the kept words; a flattened line (1 px orange core, soft glow) sweeps down as a mask edge in 0.4 s; a pressed button squashes into the next screen’s underline.
- **For Kallo:** Type “two eggs on toast with avocado”, delete the filler, and hairlines join the food words: the meal visibly breaks into parts.
- **Source:** *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:01.4. Also seen in: *Voice Agent Builder* (SpaceXAI) at 0:02.4; *Voice Agent Builder* (SpaceXAI) at 0:07.7; *Voice Agent Builder* (SpaceXAI) at 0:01.3.

#### X4 · One outline morphs into the next device

- **When to use:** Two surfaces, one object, no device renders.

#### X8 · One thing flies into the lens

### Rhythm

#### R1 · Accelerate, then stop dead

- **When to use:** Building to a reveal in under 15 s.

- **When to use:** Range without new footage.

#### R5 · A burst right before the reveal

## 5. Sound and endings

Where sound hits, and how the films sign off. 15 cards, each merged from the techniques the eight study passes recorded.

### Sound

#### S1 · Near-silence until the first result

- **When to use:** Making the output feel like an arrival.
- **Recipe:** 0–11.4 s only UI clicks and ticks (−22 to −43 dB RMS); the music hits on the frame the result appears, an ~18 dB step. Titles sit on room tone; music starts with the first UI.
- **For Kallo:** Typing and lookup on sound effects only (keys, a soft tick per matched food); the first real hit on the frame the numbers land.
- **Source:** *Claude Design* (Claude) at 0:11.6. Also seen in: *Claude connector for Blender* (Claude) at 0:08.0; *Grok 4.5* (SpaceXAI) at 0:33.0.

#### S2 · The product is the soundtrack

- **When to use:** Sound effects only, no bed.
- **Recipe:** No music. The picture moves only while the product makes sound; true silence (−80 dB) between events; one blip per action and one loudest event for the payoff.
- **For Kallo:** A key click per character, one tick per ingredient matched, one warm tone when the total lands, silence between.
- **Source:** *Voice cloning quiz* (SpaceXAI) at 0:13.0. Also seen in: *Text to Speech API* (SpaceXAI) at 0:04.8; *Fable 5 is back* (Claude) at 0:05.5.

#### S3 · Drop out for the magic, slam back on the result

- **When to use:** Marking the one moment that matters.
- **Recipe:** The bed thins or is low-passed for 3–9 s under the working moment (a pad only, the high band gone), then returns with one hit on the exact frame the result appears.
- **For Kallo:** Muffle everything while the sentence is read, then open it with one clean hit on the frame the numbers appear.
- **Source:** *Computer History* (OpenAI) at 0:10.3. Also seen in: *dots* (OpenAI) at 0:20.3; *Cowork* (Claude) at 0:36.3; *Grok in X for iOS* (SpaceXAI) at 0:18.3; *ChatGPT Work* (OpenAI) at 0:46.2.

#### S4 · A riser into a hard stop

- **When to use:** Setting up the reveal.
- **Recipe:** A continuous riser thickens as the cuts accelerate (6.4–13.05 s), then cuts to near-silence as one image holds 0.75 s; single hits land on the title and the logo.
- **For Kallo:** Layered ticks rising under the plate cuts, total silence on the held frame, one warm click as the numbers appear.
- **Source:** *Claude Fable 5* (Claude) at 0:13.3.

#### S5 · Silence for the name; one sound for the mark

- **When to use:** Landing the name.

#### S6 · Ticks as the structure

- **When to use:** An even pace with a turn.
- **Recipe:** A metronomic tick every ~0.4–0.55 s (110–113 BPM) that steps up in density at the halfway turn; a kick enters one bar before a list; the ticks stop 1.5–3 s after the call to action and a pad carries the logo.

#### S7 · Built for muted autoplay

- **When to use:** Feed teasers.
- **Recipe:** Short teasers and loops ship with no audio stream at all; every message (the build, the hold, a name at 8–13% of height held 3 s or more) reads with sound off.
